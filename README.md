## 🌟 Key Features

- **Daily Procurement Entry (பால் கொள்முதல் பதிவு)**: Fast entry for morning and evening milk collection with quantity, FAT%, SNF%, and auto-calculated rate/amount.
- **Member / Farmer Ledger (உறுப்பினர் கணக்கு)**: Easy lookup of society members with passbook and slip history.
- **Audit-Ready Day Book (தினசரி குறிப்பேடு)**: Automatically compiles daily receipts and disbursements to match dairy cooperative audit standards.
- **Bilingual Interface**: Seamless Tamil and English localization for field operators.
- **Offline Reliability**: Works directly on mobile devices without mandatory internet connection during collection hours.
